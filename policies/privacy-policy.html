HERBARYO PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 10/24/2026

At the HERBARYO Development team, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our game or visit our website.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Information You Provide Directly: Account registration details (email address, username, password) and billing/payment information when you purchase a copy of the game.
  • Information Collected Automatically: Hardware specifications, operating system, IP address, device identifiers, gameplay analytics (statistics, session length), and crash reports.
  • Communications: Chat logs, support tickets, and feedback submissions.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our games and online services.
  • Process transactions and manage your account.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent cheating, fraud, or policy violations.
  • Send you important updates, security alerts, and support messages.

3. SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers who assist us in operating our servers, processing payments, or delivering customer support (under strict confidentiality agreements).
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or law enforcement requests.

4. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on your region (such as GDPR or CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications.
  • Contact our Data Protection Officer at byry2535@gmail.com with any privacy-related questions or requests.
